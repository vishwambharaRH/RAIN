This little document is intended to be the foundation for writing the next parts of my story.
I have got as far as 6400 words without any plan or anything as such. I’ll try to make a detailed plan for around 22 chapters in the book, with an average of 2000 words per chapter, making it a total of 44,000 words, which is more than enough words for a novella, almost nearing the range of novels.

**Ch1** plot - Boy is terribly depressed. When it rains, he goes to the bus stop and sits alone. He enjoys being alone. When it's raining, a girl comes into the shelter to talk to boy. Boy feels angry at first, then talks to girl. Ends up liking talking to other people for the first time in his life. (Somehow, this was not as emotional as I intended it to be).

**Ch2** plot - Girl leaves boy alone at bus stop because she has work; boy feels it’s incomplete and goes back to the bus stop the following day and waits. His patience and luck are rewarded, as she comes, sits silently for some while.

**Ch3** plot - Girl asks boy if they could go for coffee, boy is taken aback, but he accepts. They walk on to the café nearby, and have some coffee and talk a bit. (I made the conversation between them very nicely here, but I feel it could have some more stuff about coffee, because I like coffee)

**Ch4** plot - Boy wakes up after returning from his evening’s outing, and is extremely happy, for a change. He feels that he should go out into the beautiful forests of the PN and explore. The ground is wet from the rain, but he walks quickly and reaches the top of a little hillock that overlooks the little town of KF, OR. He loves it there, amidst the peace and quiet of the forest, without any people around him. He appreciates daylight and the sun for the first time in a very long time. Finds out he likes the girl.

**Ch5** plot - Grapples with realization of the feeling of love. Boy decides to go to the town and have a bit of exploration fun, finds a library with many like-minded people. Realises that there is more to life than what he had originally thought. But his mind is still on girl.(Focus more on the town this time, give them a lot of hints about where it is, what the town is famous for. While there may not be any hostel there, there is definitely a university.)

**Ch6** plot - Boy finds girl in the town, sitting in the wood-shelved library. They sit and talk till the library closes, then they finally exchange numbers.

**Ch7** plot - Autumn arrives. Boy walks through the woods, marvelling at the vivid reds, oranges, and yellows. The air is crisp, and the bus stop feels different in this season. Girl comments on how fleeting it all feels, which sparks a conversation about change and how neither of them feels entirely in control of their lives. They part with an unspoken understanding that both are searching for something deeper.

**Ch8** plot - Girl begins to pull away a little, and boy notices but doesn't know how to address it. The autumn winds grow colder, reflecting the subtle shift in their dynamic. They meet less frequently at the bus stop, and when they do, girl seems more distant, distracted by thoughts she doesn’t share. Boy tries to enjoy the beauty of the season, but it feels incomplete without her presence.

**Ch9** plot - The first frost appears. Boy, hoping to reignite their connection, suggests they go for a walk again, but girl declines. Her reasons are vague, and boy starts to worry that perhaps something has shifted irreversibly. He spends time alone in the woods, kicking fallen leaves and contemplating whether he’s imagined the bond they’ve built. The chapter ends with him deciding to give her space but unsure if that’s the right choice.

**Ch10** plot - Autumn is in full swing. Boy sits at the bus stop more frequently, as the leaves continue to fall, covering the streets. On one particularly windy day, she shows up unexpectedly. They talk, but this time the conversation is stilted. She hints at something in her life that she hasn't shared yet. There’s a tension, but also the sense that they’re both holding back. The chapter ends with the promise of winter, the world around them growing colder.

**Ch11** plot - Winter sets in, and the first snowfall arrives. Boy finds himself at the bus stop, waiting, hoping she’ll show up. The world around him is transformed, blanketed in white. He catches snowflakes on his fingertips, feeling a strange sense of peace despite girl’s absence. Later that evening, she texts him for the first time. It’s brief, but she asks him to meet her in town for coffee. Boy feels a glimmer of hope and excitement, like maybe things can return to what they were.

**Ch12** plot - The coffee shop meeting feels different - warmer, more intimate. They talk for hours, the window fogging up from the warmth inside as snow piles up outside. Girl shares something personal for the first time, revealing part of why she’s been distant. Boy listens intently, realising how little he knows about her life. There’s an unspoken agreement between them that things are changing, growing deeper. The chapter ends with them parting ways, both feeling closer yet still unsure of where things are headed.

**Ch13** plot - The snow grows heavier as winter tightens its grip. Boy and girl meet regularly now, their connection feeling stronger. They walk through the snowy woods, their conversations flowing more easily. Boy notices she laughs more, relaxes more in his presence, but there’s still a guarded part of her that he can’t quite reach. He’s hesitant to push, but he starts to realise that he cares for her more than he initially thought. She, too, seems to be warming to the idea of something more between them, though neither of them says it out loud yet.

**Ch14** plot - As winter deepens, so does boy’s growing awareness of his feelings for girl. He finds himself wanting to spend more time with her, even though they haven’t defined their relationship. She surprises him by inviting him to an art exhibit in town. They walk through the gallery together, discussing life, dreams, and the future. Boy senses a shift in her, something softer, more open. That night, as they say goodbye, she holds his gaze a little longer than usual. The tension between them is palpable, but neither makes a move—yet. The chapter ends with boy standing in the snow, watching her walk away, wondering if he’ll ever have the courage to tell her how he really feels. (holding hands in the snow, perhaps having a warm beer together, basically really damn romantic without full blown open romance)

**Ch15** plot - Winter is at its peak. Boy and girl now meet regularly, and the once-awkward silences have transformed into comfortable pauses between conversations. One evening, they decide to walk around town after coffee. The streets are empty, covered in a blanket of snow. They talk about their lives, their pasts, their families. Most open that girl has ever been. As they walk, their shoulders brush, and boy feels an unfamiliar but welcome warmth. They stop by a frozen fountain, and in the quiet of the night, he almost confesses his feelings, but hesitates, letting the moment pass.


**Ch16** plot - Boy struggles with his growing feelings. He tries to act normal, but every moment they spend together makes it harder to hold back. Winter begins to wane, and on one particularly cold afternoon, they visit a local bookstore. The cosy interior, the smell of old books, and the faint sounds of the bustling street outside give them a sense of escape. Girl picks out a book and recommends it to boy, and they sit in a quiet corner, reading together. There’s a closeness between them that’s never been spoken but is undeniable. The chapter ends with boy holding the book, wondering if the next chapter of their relationship will finally unfold.

**Ch17** plot - The first signs of spring appear. The snow begins to melt, revealing patches of green beneath the white. Boy and girl take their first walk in the woods since winter began. The trees are still bare, but the air feels different—warmer, promising something new. They talk about the future, their hopes, their fears. Boy is tempted to ask if she feels the same way he does, but he still holds back, unsure if he’s ready for the answer. The chapter ends with them sitting by the waterfall they discovered earlier, watching the ice slowly melt away, much like the distance between them.

**Ch18** plot - Spring begins to bloom. The flowers in the town park start to blossom, and the air smells fresh and new. Boy is more certain of his feelings now. He knows he can’t keep pretending they’re just friends, but every time he tries to tell her, something holds him back. They meet at the coffee shop again, sitting by the misty window, watching the rain turn to a light drizzle. She seems different too, more open, more vulnerable. They share stories they’ve never told anyone else before, but the conversation still dances around their unspoken feelings. The chapter ends with a brief, tender touch—a hand on a shoulder, a soft smile—and boy feels a shift, like something between them has finally changed.

**Ch19** plot - As spring takes over, the town transforms. The trees burst with leaves, and the scent of flowers fills the air. Boy and girl take long walks through the woods, their talks becoming deeper, more introspective. Boy notices how she seems lighter, less burdened by whatever had held her back before. One day, they sit by the waterfall again, and this time, boy can’t hold back any longer. He starts to confess his feelings but is interrupted by a sudden downpour. They laugh, running through the rain, soaked but smiling. The tension breaks, and they share a moment that feels like the beginning of something more, but the confession remains unsaid.

**Ch20** plot - The rain clears, and with it, the uncertainty between them seems to lift. They sit in the coffee shop again, this time with more ease between them. Girl talks about her future, her plans, and for the first time, boy realises she might not always be around. This thought stirs something in him, an urgency he hadn’t felt before. He wants to tell her how he feels, but he’s afraid of what her plans mean for them. The chapter ends with them walking in the park, the blossoms above them in full bloom, and boy wondering if he’s waited too long to say anything.

**Ch21** plot - Spring deepens, and the town is alive with colour. Boy and girl spend almost every day together now. One afternoon, they visit an art exhibit in a nearby city. As they walk through the gallery, discussing the pieces, boy realises how much she’s changed him. He’s no longer the lonely, sad person he was when they first met. He knows he has to tell her how he feels, but as they sit in a quiet café afterward, she drops a bombshell—she’s considering leaving town for a new opportunity. The chapter ends with boy grappling with the news, unsure of what to do next.

**Ch22** plot - The days grow warmer, and boy’s feelings of uncertainty grow with them. He knows she could leave any day now, and he hasn’t said anything about how he feels. They meet one last time at the bus stop, just like they did in the beginning. The rain returns, falling gently as they stand together. This time, boy doesn’t hesitate. He tells her everything—how much she means to him, how she’s changed his life. She listens quietly, and for a moment, he fears the worst. But then she smiles, and without saying anything, she takes his hand. They stand in the rain, the weight of unspoken words finally lifting.
Boy and girl finally accept their true feelings for each other and end up finally sharing an intimate kiss.

I’m an old fashioned person believing in the purity of love and the importance of physical touch. Something that is physical is equally as important as something mental, if not more.
Your body is a vessel for your mind. Even if your mind is dull, your body can still be used some way or the other. But if it’s the other way, it’s a curse. You will never be able to experience stuff for yourself.
That is why I’m treating this intimate physical touch as something so important.
