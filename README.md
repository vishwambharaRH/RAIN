# RAIN
This repository is meant to serve as a common base for my writing project, a small 40-44k word novella.

There are already 4 posts up on this in my blog, [Pondersome Ponderings](pondersomeponderings.blogspot.com), hence if you try something as stupid as copying somebody's stuff, I will come after you, whoever you are. For goodness' sake, I will put in a license here too. 

I am also removing previous AI-made artwork that I used on the blog. I will draw the pictures myself, two images per chapter.

The purpose of this book is to test my limits on romantic writing, as it's usually the 7-foot werewolf vampires that sadly hijack the modern discourse on it. True love, as such, is not the kind of "animal" attraction one sees in bookTok in/femcels. Everybody wants to have their ways in their relationships, I do not judge them for that, but I do judge it when the question of certain unconsented action appears in these books. It's a horrible thing to see, especially given that these writers peddle to the impressionable young crowd of Gen Alphas, Betas and whatnot. Do they intend to create perverted humans? Perhaps not. But they must understand their positions of power in the modern world, as responsible human beings. Life is short, but not **that** short.

Hence, I try this balancing act. Let's see what I can whip up.
